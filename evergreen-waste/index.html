Good questions

Frequently asked.

What area do you service?

We serve New Castle County, Delaware — Wilmington, Newark, Bear, New Castle, Middletown and the surrounding communities. Not sure about your street? Pop your ZIP in at the top of the page or just ask our assistant.

Do you offer different bin sizes?

Yes. Residential carts come in a few sizes for both trash and recycling, so you're not paying for space you don't need — or cramming into one that's too small. Our team helps you pick the right fit when you start.

How soon can I start service?

Usually within a few days of signing up. Once your address is confirmed and your plan is set, we schedule cart delivery and your pickup day — often before your next scheduled service.

How do payments work?

Flexible plans: pay monthly, quarterly, or yearly (paying ahead saves the most). You can manage billing online anytime, and our team is a call away for anything billing-related.

Is my pickup delayed for holidays?

Holidays can push your pickup by a day. We keep the banner at the top of this page current — and our assistant always knows the latest, so you can just ask "is my trash delayed this week?"
